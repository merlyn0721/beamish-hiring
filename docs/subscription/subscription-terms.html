Employer Subscription Terms & Conditions

DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW — demonstration content only. Not a final or enforceable Beamish customer agreement.

1. Purpose and status

DRAFT FOR LEGAL AND COMMERCIAL REVIEW. This document explains how a Beamish employer account and any paid subscription could operate. This sample does not establish a final contract or activate paid services.

2. Free employer account

An employer may register for a Free account and prepare a company profile, submit verification information and prepare hiring drafts. Access to job publication and premium tools depends on published plan entitlements and verification.

3. Company verification

Beamish may request SSM registration information, company profile documents and applicable business licences. Approval of a business account is separate from purchasing a subscription. Verification badges must not be purchased.

4. Plans and included benefits

Available features, job posting slots, team seats, profile views, boost credits, AI tools and analytics depend on the current plan schedule. Employers should review the plan benefits at checkout and in My Subscription. Unused benefits may expire or reset under the published plan schedule.

5. Payment choices

Options may include month-to-month billing, annual payment in full, prepaid multi-month packages and approved annual instalments. Instalments are a payment schedule for an agreed term, not automatically a monthly cancellable subscription. Final availability is subject to provider and credit approval. Taxes and transaction fees, if applicable, must be disclosed before acceptance.

6. Activation and renewal

A paid plan should begin only after payment authorisation and recorded acceptance of the applicable terms. Renewal choices, automatic renewal (if offered), renewal dates and notice periods must be clearly stated during checkout.

7. Cancellation, refunds and changes

Cancellation, refunds, proration, downgrades, suspension and disputes follow the separate Billing, Cancellation & Refund Policy, subject to applicable law. Existing entitlements and already-paid terms should not be silently changed.

8. Acceptable use and communications

Employers must not misuse applicant data, send spam, misrepresent roles or use restricted discriminatory selection criteria. Messaging and portfolio access must comply with platform privacy controls and applicable legal obligations.

9. Privacy and records

Only authorised staff should access identity, business-registration, billing, and contractual records. Employers should be able to view applicable agreement version, approval date and acceptance history.

10. Applicable law and final approval

Governing law, dispute processes, company legal identity, final service specifications and commercial terms remain to be completed and approved by Malaysian legal counsel before production use.
