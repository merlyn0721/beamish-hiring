Cancellation, Refunds & Plan Changes Policy

DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW — demonstration content only. Not a final or enforceable Beamish customer agreement.

1. Status

DRAFT. Review with legal, finance and operations before publication. No rights are waived by this sample.

2. Cancellation requests

Allow employers to see their current plan, next renewal date and the expected effect of cancellation. A cancellation request should be recorded and acknowledged.

3. Monthly subscriptions

Specify whether cancellation stops the next renewal or takes effect immediately. Clarify any minimum term, final bill and whether unused allowances remain active through the paid period.

4. Annual upfront

Show whether early termination is allowed, and any proration or refund conditions. These must be agreed explicitly and comply with applicable consumer and commercial law.

5. Annual instalments

Clarify that an annual contract paid by instalments may still involve an outstanding contractual balance, subject to approval and applicable law. It must not be marketed as a no-commitment monthly plan.

6. Service failures and disputes

Explain how duplicate charges, service non-provision, disputed payments and erroneous billing are investigated, corrected and refunded where required.

7. Downgrade and benefits

Explain what happens to live job slots, team seats, boost credits and analytics at downgrade or expiration, including notice and preservation of employer data.

8. Finance and audit

Finance manages refund decisions, invoices and settlements. Platform Admin manages service access and approval rules. Changes should maintain a timestamped audit trail.
