Billing & Payment Schedule

DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW — demonstration content only. Not a final or enforceable Beamish customer agreement.

1. Status

DRAFT. Commercial terms and figures in this HTML prototype are illustrative. An actual payment provider is not connected.

2. Monthly billing

Billed every month for the selected plan. Show full amount due, billing start date, taxes, renewal terms and cancellation treatment before confirmation.

3. Annual upfront

Twelve-month entitlement term settled in one upfront payment, with any approved annual discount disclosed. Renewal should not be assumed without explicit disclosure.

4. Annual instalments

An annual commitment payable in monthly instalments only where approved. Show total contracted amount, number of instalments, due dates, provider eligibility, missed-payment treatment and cancellation liabilities.

5. Multi-month prepaid

A fixed number of months prepaid with any discount specified. The start/end dates and policy on early cancellation should be clear.

6. Invoices and records

Every completed transaction should create a receipt or invoice, link it to subscription and employer records, and keep payment and subscription status as separate attributes.

7. Plan changes and tax

Upgrades, downgrades, discounts, tax and currency rules are managed by Finance Admin, but plan entitlements are managed by Platform Admin. CMS controls only public-facing copy and layout.
